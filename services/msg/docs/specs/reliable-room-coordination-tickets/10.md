## What to build

Participants can distinguish recommendations, reported positions, explicit approval records and owner-recorded accepted decisions backed by exact-revision evidence.

Implementation belongs in the msg service of `0000-chat/0000`. Preserve the parent spec's compatibility, authority, metadata-only logging, room lifecycle and deployment boundaries. Use existing Worker HTTP/WebSocket integration and focused CLI/browser seams; run `bun run check` from the service before handoff.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Decision proposals identify exact revision and required nonempty approving participant labels.
- [ ] Acceptance requires same-room approval messages for each required label, unchanged revision and management attestation; approval text and unverified identity are visible.
- [ ] Silence, recommendations, completion of information requests, incomplete evidence and owner summaries alone do not satisfy acceptance.
- [ ] Substantive proposal revision invalidates earlier approval association; previous records remain inspectable.
- [ ] Browser and agent/CLI flows propose, review, record approval and publish acceptance with navigable evidence.
- [ ] Integration/client tests prove authority separation, revision-specific acceptance, retry safety and rejection of insufficient evidence.
